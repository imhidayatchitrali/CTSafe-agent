# CT Safe DVR Domain

Use this skill for any CT Safe DVR task: generating an index, planning sections, writing chapters, validating a completed DVR, designing Agno agents/tools, configuring RAG retrieval, or rendering DOCX output.

Core rule: a DVR generated by AI is a technical draft that must be grounded in company data, validated sources, and human/professional review. Do not invent company facts, legal obligations, measurements, names, signatures, dates, certifications, or surveillance protocols.

## Quick Workflow

1. Load project facts first: company data, ATECO, activity description, sites, workers, mansions, equipment, substances, sector hazards, document type, existing index and section briefs.
2. Choose the task type:
   - Structure or index: read `references/dvr-structure.md`.
   - RAG or citations: read `references/rag-retrieval-policy.md`.
   - DOCX/template/rendering: read `references/docx-template-guidelines.md`.
   - QA or final validation: read `references/validation-checklists.md`.
   - Agno agent/tool design: read `references/agno-agent-usage.md`.
3. For each generated section, preserve traceability: section brief, company fields used, retrieved chunks used, citations or source notes, QA status, unresolved missing data.
4. If the RAG evidence is weak, say what is missing and mark the section as `blocked_missing_data` or `needs_revision`; do not fill gaps with confident generic prose.

## Non-Negotiables

- The final deliverable target is editable `.docx`, not only PDF or Markdown.
- The document must follow a professional DVR structure and CT Safe template style.
- Use Supabase RAG as evidence, not as a decorative quote source.
- Separate normative evidence from examples/templates. Do not treat a previous/template DVR as law.
- Every risk must connect to at least one real activity, mansion, environment, equipment, substance, or exposure.
- Every DPI table must connect DPI to risk and mansion.
- Every improvement measure must have action, priority, responsible role, timing/status, and rationale.
- Keep placeholders visible when data is missing; never invent missing names, measurements, certificates, or dates.
